About This Resource

A Shopping Brief method and storefront grid built around a shared EastMallBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use an EastMallBuy spreadsheet. It offers a Shopping Brief method — product type, intended use, must-haves, preferences, and open questions — so each candidate is measured against your own written needs before any comparison is made.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official EastMallBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by EastMallBuy or Google. It is not a store, a link converter, a QC database, a price tracker, a shipping calculator, an order tracking tool, a budget tool, or a QC certification service.

The Shopping Brief method

Everything here follows five fields: Product type (what you are looking for), Intended use (the real purpose), Must have (non-negotiable conditions), Prefer (nice-to-have, not required), and Still need to verify (open questions to re-check). A low price, a pretty picture, or a popular label is never treated as proof that a candidate fits.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product category windows from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any comparison.
  4. Write your brief, set stop rules, and re-check the destination page.
