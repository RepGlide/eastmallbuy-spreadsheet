Shopping Brief Guide

Write the product type, intended use, must-haves, preferences, and open questions before browsing.

A brief is written before the click

A Shopping Brief is a short plan you write before you open any listing. Its job is to hold your own requirements in place so that a bright display — a low price, a glossy photo, a popular label — cannot quietly become your reason for choosing. The brief, not the storefront, decides what belongs.

Field 1 — Product type

Name exactly what you are looking for. “A bag” is not a product type; “a structured crossbody bag that closes with a flap” is. If you cannot name the type, you are not ready to browse yet.

Field 2 — Intended use

Write the real purpose the item will serve: a daily commute, a weekend trip, a gift, a specific occasion. Use is what turns a list of features into a set of requirements. Two items can look alike and serve completely different purposes.

Field 3 — Must have

List the conditions that cannot be dropped. Size, material, a closing mechanism, a minimum width — whatever is non-negotiable. Keep this list short and concrete, because every must-have is a gate a candidate has to pass.

Field 4 — Prefer

Record what would be nice but is not required: a color, a lining, a brand, a finish. Preferences only break a tie between two candidates that both pass every must-have. A preference never overrides a missing must-have.

Field 5 — Still need to verify

Keep the questions you have to re-confirm on the current page: strap length, hardware, exact fit, what is actually included. Write these down as open questions so they are answered deliberately instead of guessed.

Brief ruleA low price, a pretty picture, or a popular label cannot prove a candidate fits your needs. Only a written must-have can gate a candidate.

A worked brief

  1. Product type: a structured crossbody bag with a flap closure.
  2. Intended use: daily commute, carrying a small tablet and essentials.
  3. Must have: fits a 13″ laptop, adjustable strap, closes with a flap.
  4. Prefer: water-resistant, black, one interior pocket.
  5. Still verify: strap length range and whether the strap is included.
